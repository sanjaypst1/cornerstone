Placement model · mixed Manager+ audience

Not Everyone in the Same Workshop

Design rule: do not seat all 35 in one classroom. Diagnose first. Title does not equal product proficiency.

How CORNERSTONE Places People

  • • Baseline scenarios mapped to the 6 Ops steps
  • • Sponsor / manager input on current practice
  • • Check prior PO training vs actual application
  • • Assign Foundation / Intermediate / Advanced
  • • Route into Value in Practice and/or Outcomes Leadership

Indicative Split of ~35

  • • Foundation: ~12–14 → Value in Practice
  • • Intermediate: ~12–14 → Value in Practice + selected Outcomes modules
  • • Advanced: ~8–10 → Outcomes Leadership
  • • Optional 90-min shared Orient kickoff on the 6-step cycle

Measuring CORNERSTONE value · product & operational metrics

How We Prove Value Is Coming Out of the Model

CORNERSTONE is not measured by training attendance alone. We track product performance and operational delivery metrics that show whether Product Ownership is creating clearer value, better flow, and stronger outcomes across the Product Management Operations cycle.

Product Performance Metrics

  • • Value proposition clarity score (before / after)
  • • % roadmap items framed as outcomes vs output/projects
  • • Adoption / usage signals on priority products
  • • Customer feedback / VoC items closed into backlog
  • • Client experience improvements linked to product decisions

Delivery & Operational Metrics

  • • Cycle time from idea to validated decision to delivery slice
  • • Predictability of weekly / monthly / quarterly goal completion
  • • Backlog health: % refined, ordered, and ready
  • • Cross-team coordination lead time (product and partner teams)
  • • Reduced rework caused by unclear ownership or priorities

CORNERSTONE Capability Metrics

  • • % colleagues advancing at least one proficiency level
  • • % at Intermediate / Advanced after Enact phase
  • • % completing live ownership artifacts
  • • Manager confirmation: can run PO rituals unaided
  • • 90-day sustainment of CORNERSTONE rituals in BAU
